## 💡 The Idea

Manual bottling is slow and inconsistent: every bottle ends up with a slightly different amount of liquid.

This graduation project solves that with a **PLC-controlled production line** that fills, caps, and packages bottles automatically, with consistent quality and minimal human intervention.

## 🎯 My Role: Precision Filling

I focused on the **heart of the line: the filling stage**.

> **Target:** a filling error of **2% or less**, so every bottle gets the right amount, every time.

I worked on tuning and testing the filling process, sensor placement, and flow control to get consistent, repeatable volumes.

## ⚙️ How the Line Works

| Stage | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🍼 **1. Filling** | Capacitive sensor detects the bottle, solenoid valve & pump activate, and the target volume is dispensed |
| 🔒 **2. Capping** | The cap is placed and sealed |
| 📦 **3. Packaging** | Finished bottles are organized and ready for delivery |
